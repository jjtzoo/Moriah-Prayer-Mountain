# Moriah Prayer Mountain — Post-Inquiry Operations Plan

**Status:** Proposed manual workflow for ministry review. These steps describe an operating process; they do not set approved rental prices, payment deadlines, refund terms, donation claims, or legal/tax status.

## 1. Purpose and boundaries

This plan starts after someone submits the website inquiry form or contacts Moriah directly. Staff handle follow-up outside the website using a ministry-controlled inbox, a private booking register, and separate donation records.

There are two independent tracks:

1. **Paid services:** room stays, chapel use, and function hall rentals. A request is not a reservation; a quote is not a payment; a payment is not a confirmed booking until Moriah verifies it and sends written confirmation.
2. **Voluntary gifts:** donation questions and completed gifts. A question is not a gift. Do not use the rental invoice or booking register for donations, or the donation link for fees.

The website's donation/support inquiry only asks a question. It does not collect money. Inquiry delivery also depends on configuring the website's Resend email service; confirm that messages reach the ministry inbox before relying on the form.

## 2. Recommended operating model

Start with a small manual process rather than building a booking system before Moriah has settled inventory, pricing, and policies.

- **One official ministry inbox** receives website inquiries and direct messages. Only authorized staff can access it.
- **One booking register** records room, chapel, and function hall requests and reservations. Keep access limited to the staff who need it.
- **One donation register** records gifts separately from paid services and records each transfer against the receiving account's transaction record.
- **One named inquiry coordinator** owns triage, acknowledgments, follow-up dates, and status updates.
- **A facility/operations contact** confirms capacity, room setup, availability, and venue requirements.
- **A treasurer or finance contact** verifies incoming payments, issues or checks invoices, handles refunds through the approved process, and reconciles account records.
- **A ministry approver** approves prices, exceptions, cancellation terms, donation purposes, and public fundraising claims.

At the beginning, one person may cover more than one role. Keep payment confirmation and refund approval visible to a second authorized person whenever practical.

## 3. Intake and response standards

### Triage

At least once each working day, the inquiry coordinator should:

1. Check the inbox, spam folder, and any designated social-message channel.
2. Create a unique reference, such as `MPM-2026-0001`.
3. Classify the request as **Room stay**, **Chapel rental**, **Function hall rental**, **Donation question**, or **General**.
4. Record the receipt date, channel, owner, current status, and next follow-up date.
5. Acknowledge receipt, ideally within one working day. If a full answer needs more research, give the visitor an expected response date.

Use a draft target of **two working days for a substantive first answer**, excluding weekends and local holidays. This is an internal service goal, not a promise shown to visitors until the ministry approves staffing and coverage.

### Ask only for missing information

- **Room stay:** arrival/departure, number of guests, room preference if known, number of rooms if known, and contact details.
- **Chapel or function hall:** event type, preferred date/time, approximate attendance, setup needs, and a contact person.
- **Donation question:** the question or intended giving purpose. Do not ask for payment details in the inquiry form or email.
- Avoid collecting guest lists, IDs, health details, or other sensitive information before there is a clear operational need and an approved way to protect it.

## 4. Paid-service workflow

### A. Inquiry to availability check

1. Acknowledge the inquiry and confirm the requested service and dates.
2. Check one ministry-owned calendar or booking register before discussing a hold. Check room inventory or venue scheduling with the designated operations contact.
3. If details are missing, ask one consolidated set of questions and set a follow-up date.
4. If the date is unavailable, offer alternatives only after confirming them.
5. Do not promise room capacity, configuration, equipment, meals, access, or availability that has not been confirmed.

### B. Quote and provisional hold

Send a written quote that clearly states:

- guest/event name and reference number;
- service, confirmed date and time, and approved room/space arrangement;
- itemized charges and total in PHP, with any other currency terms if approved;
- what is included and excluded;
- full-payment amount and deadline, if the ministry chooses a full-payment model;
- the date and time any provisional hold expires;
- cancellation, refund, date-change, and no-show terms;
- how the visitor can accept the quote and how to contact Moriah with corrections.

**Proposed pilot rule for ministry approval:** after operations confirms availability and the visitor agrees to the written quote, place a short, explicit provisional hold (for example, three working days) while payment is arranged. State the precise expiry in the quote. Until approved, treat this as a decision to make—not an active policy.

Other centers use explicit, written holds and acceptance steps, but their terms differ. Christian Renewal Center describes quotes, a 14-day hold when a contract is sent, and a signed contract plus deposit; Williamsburg Christian Retreat Center describes a one-week hold and says the signed contract and deposit complete the reservation. These examples support clear deadlines and written acceptance, not copying their amounts or durations.

### C. Acceptance, payment, and confirmation

1. For a function hall event or a multi-room/group stay, use an approved written agreement or booking confirmation that captures the service, total, schedule, included setup, and cancellation terms.
2. Send a PayPal invoice or payment request only after the service, dates, and total are agreed. Identify it as a **room-stay payment**, **chapel rental**, or **function hall rental**, never as a donation.
3. If Moriah proceeds with the requested full-payment model, set the full amount and deadline in the approved quote/agreement. Do not collect payment before availability and the total are confirmed.
4. Check the receiving PayPal account or its transaction activity directly. Do not mark a booking paid based only on an email, screenshot, or visitor statement. Resolve pending, held, duplicate, or disputed transactions with the finance contact before confirming.
5. Once payment is verified and all booking conditions are met, change the register to **Confirmed** and send a confirmation containing the reference number, dates, service, agreed amount, payment status, arrival/event instructions, and a contact for changes.
6. If the hold expires without the required acceptance/payment, tell the visitor the hold expired, release the date, and mark the record **Expired**. Do not silently keep inventory blocked.

PayPal Philippines describes invoices as secure links that can be emailed or shared, with payment status visible in the PayPal account. This makes invoicing a practical manual step for agreed rental charges; it does not replace Moriah's availability check, written terms, or reconciliation.

### D. Before arrival/event and closeout

- Reconfirm arrival or event details on a ministry-approved schedule.
- For groups, set a due date for final headcount, room allocation, setup, and other approved operational details.
- Record additional charges only if the agreement explains how they are handled and an authorized contact approves them.
- After the stay/event, mark the request **Completed**, reconcile the final amount, and record any approved refund or adjustment with its reference and approver.

## 5. Donation-question and gift workflow

### A. Donation question

1. Route it to the **Donation question** category and acknowledge it separately from rental requests.
2. Answer using ministry-approved wording about the purpose, receiving organization/account, and intended use of funds.
3. If Moriah has not approved a campaign or verified a receiving account, say that giving details are not yet available. Do not imply that sending the form made a donation.
4. Never send a donation link in response to a room/venue quote or use a donation checkout to collect service fees.

### B. Before publishing a donation link

The ministry should approve and document:

- the legal/organizational recipient and the authorized account owner;
- the giving purpose and whether gifts are general or restricted to a named campaign;
- who can access the account and donor records;
- how funds will be withdrawn, safeguarded, used, and reported;
- who answers donor questions and handles corrections/refunds;
- the exact language allowed on the website, acknowledgments, and campaign page.

PayPal Philippines says charity confirmation requires evidence of legal registration and representative authority; its Philippines help page lists a BIR Tax Exemption Certificate or NGO Certificate of Registration issued by the BIR, with the organization's name and validity period shown. Confirm current requirements directly with PayPal for the specific receiving account before publishing a Donate button. Do not claim tax deductibility unless Moriah's status and the applicable rules have been verified by an appropriate local adviser.

### C. Gift receipt and acknowledgment

1. Use a dedicated PayPal Donate link/button or approved campaign page once the organization and account are verified. Keep it separate from the rental payment method and wording.
2. Check the account transaction record, not just a donor's screenshot or email.
3. Record the transaction ID, date, gross amount, currency, fees/net amount if available, campaign or restriction, and acknowledgment status in the donation register.
4. Send an approved thank-you/acknowledgment. Issue a formal receipt only if the ministry has approved the format and it is appropriate for its status.
5. Reconcile gifts to PayPal statements and bank withdrawals on a fixed cadence, such as monthly. Keep donation totals out of the rental revenue report.

## 6. Statuses and minimum records

### Booking register statuses

`New → Acknowledged → Needs details → Checking availability → Quote sent → Hold until [date/time] → Awaiting payment → Confirmed → Completed`

Alternative close states: `Unavailable`, `Declined`, `Expired`, `Cancelled`, or `Refund pending/processed`.

Each booking record should include: reference, service type, contact details needed to reply, requested and confirmed dates, guest/event count, confirmed space/setup, assigned owner, quote/agreement reference, total and currency, hold expiry, payment reference/status, cancellation/change outcome, and next action/date.

### Donation register statuses

`Question received → Answered → Awaiting approved giving details → Giving link sent → Gift verified → Acknowledged → Reconciled`

Alternative close states: `No gift made`, `Anonymous gift`, `Unmatched transaction`, or `Refund/adjustment review`.

Each donation record should include only the donor/contact information available and needed, transaction reference, date, gross/currency/fees/net where available, approved purpose or restriction, receiving account, acknowledgment status, and reconciliation date. Do not record card numbers, passwords, one-time codes, or bank login data.

## 7. Inbox labels and response templates

Use mailbox labels that mirror the service types and status. Keep donation messages visibly distinct from booking messages. Templates should be reviewed and approved before use.

### Acknowledgment

> Thank you for contacting Moriah Prayer Mountain. We received your [room/chapel/function hall/support] inquiry, reference [ID]. We’re checking the details and expect to reply by [date]. This message does not confirm a reservation or record a donation.

### Quote and hold

> We can offer [service/space] on [date] for [approved arrangement] at a total of [amount/currency]. The quote includes [items]. The provisional hold expires [date/time, timezone]. To accept, reply confirming the details and follow the attached payment instructions. The reservation is confirmed only after Moriah verifies the required payment and sends written confirmation. [Approved cancellation/change terms.]

### Donation question while giving is not ready

> Thank you for asking how to support Moriah Prayer Mountain. We are confirming the ministry's approved giving purpose and receiving account. This inquiry has not made or recorded a donation. We’ll share verified giving details when they are ready.

### Payment confirmation

> Moriah has verified payment for reference [ID]. Your [stay/event/space] is confirmed for [date/time] under the agreed details. Please contact [ministry contact] if your plans change.

## 8. Exceptions and escalation

- **Two requests for the same dates:** operations decides based on the approved booking register/hold policy; notify both people promptly and do not promise the same room/space twice.
- **Unavailable or unconfirmed capacity:** offer a waitlist or alternate dates only if staff can maintain it reliably; otherwise close clearly as unavailable.
- **Payment pending, held, duplicated, or disputed:** leave status as **Awaiting payment** or **Finance review**; finance checks the account and decides the next step.
- **Cancellation, refund, or date change:** use only the approved published terms; record the request, decision, amount, transaction reference, and approver.
- **Donation restriction or earmark:** accept only restrictions the ministry has agreed it can honor; otherwise explain available approved purposes before the donor gives.
- **Unmatched or anonymous gift:** finance records it as unmatched/anonymous and reconciles it to the account; do not assign a donor or purpose by guessing.
- **Urgent safety/accessibility request:** route to the designated ministry contact and collect only the information needed to respond.
- **No delivery from the website:** provide the visitor's contact email/phone as a fallback, investigate the email service, and manually create a register entry if the message reached another approved channel.

## 9. Privacy, access, and records

- Use a ministry-controlled inbox and account; avoid making one staff member's personal PayPal the public receiving account.
- Grant inbox, booking register, donation register, and payment-account access only to named people who need it. Remove access promptly when a person's role ends.
- Do not send or store full card data, PayPal passwords, authentication codes, or bank credentials. Use the payment provider's hosted invoice/donation pages.
- Keep booking and donor records in separate restricted files or separate access-controlled areas. Do not publish a public spreadsheet.
- Decide a retention period and deletion process with the ministry's records/accounting adviser; avoid keeping unsuccessful inquiries indefinitely.
- Keep a simple change history for booking dates, amounts, payment status, refunds, and donation classifications.

## 10. Weekly and monthly operations

### Daily/each working day

- Check new messages and overdue follow-ups.
- Assign an owner and next action to every open request.
- Check upcoming holds and send expiry notices before release when practical.

### Weekly

- Review all open inquiries, pending quotes, expiring holds, unpaid accepted quotes, event readiness, and unresolved finance items.
- Confirm the booking register matches the ministry's calendar.
- Escalate any request without an owner or next action.

### Monthly

- Reconcile rental transactions and refunds against PayPal and bank records.
- Separately reconcile donation transactions, fees, withdrawals, and any designated-purpose totals.
- Review response times, conversion from quote to confirmed booking, cancellations, unresolved inquiries, and unmatched gifts.
- Limit reports to aggregate information wherever individual names are not needed.

## 11. Rollout checklist

### Phase 1 — Decide and prepare

- Name the inquiry coordinator, operations contact, finance contact, and final approver.
- Verify the public contact details and configure/test inquiry email delivery.
- Confirm the ministry-owned rental receiving account and donation receiving account are authorized and controlled by the ministry.
- Approve inventory, rate basis, quote format, full-payment deadline, hold duration, cancellation/refund/date-change rules, and event agreement needs.
- Approve donation purpose, recipient, account, account eligibility, fund-use explanation, and donor acknowledgment wording.
- Create separate private booking and donation registers and a single calendar/register staff will treat as authoritative.

### Phase 2 — Run a staff-only dry run

Walk through one room inquiry, one function hall inquiry, one donation question, one successful payment, one expired hold, one cancellation/refund, and one unmatched gift. Verify that staff can identify the owner, next action, payment status, and record location without relying on the website to manage bookings.

### Phase 3 — Soft launch

- Start with a small number of real inquiries and have a second staff member review quotes and payment confirmations.
- Check the inbox daily and reconcile payments at least weekly during the initial period.
- Review response times, visitor confusion, quote corrections, and record gaps after the first month.
- Update the public site only after the underlying policy, accounts, and staff process are ready.

### Phase 4 — Decide whether to automate

Consider booking software or a custom reservation system only when staff can explain the approved inventory, pricing, hold, payment, cancellation, and confirmation rules consistently. Automation should encode an already-working process rather than invent those policies.

## 12. Decisions for ministry approval

1. Who owns the official inbox and who is the backup?
2. What response time can staff reliably meet?
3. What are the approved room, chapel, and function hall rates, inclusions, capacities, and availability rules?
4. Does Moriah want full payment before confirmation, and what deadline/hold period applies?
5. What written agreement is needed for weddings, conferences, and group stays?
6. What cancellation, refund, rescheduling, no-show, and additional-charge terms apply?
7. Which ministry entity/account receives rentals, and which verified account receives gifts?
8. What donation purposes are approved, and can gifts be restricted to one of them?
9. What acknowledgments/receipts can Moriah issue, and what tax language is permitted?
10. Who approves exceptions and reconciles PayPal to bank records?

## 13. Reference patterns reviewed

These providers publish their own policies. Their deposit amounts and deadlines are examples only and are not proposed Moriah terms.

- [Christian Renewal Center — Retreat Planning](https://www.retreatcentercrc.org/retreat-planning): quote turnaround, a clearly timed provisional hold, written agreement, and deposit/payment steps.
- [Williamsburg Christian Retreat Center — Planning Reservations](https://www.wcrc.info/blog/planning-your-retreat-101-navigating-reservations/): request enough details for a quote; reservation becomes complete only after signed terms and deposit.
- [Williamsburg Christian Retreat Center — FAQs](https://www.wcrc.info/retreats/faqs/): group-specific quote, deposit, final guest-count milestone, and event preparation.
- [Cedar Springs Christian Retreat Center — Booking Process](https://www.cedarspringscrc.org/book-your-stay-1): confirm dates and lodging, issue a contract and deposit instructions, then send reservation confirmation after both are received.
- [PayPal Philippines — Invoicing](https://www.paypal.com/ph/business/accept-payments/invoice): secure invoice links, shared links/QR codes, and payment-status tracking for service charges.
- [PayPal Philippines — Charity confirmation requirements](https://www.paypal.com/ph/cshelp/article/what-information-do-i-need-to-provide-to-confirm-my-charity-status-help223): organization and representative verification, including the listed Philippines documentation requirements.
- [PayPal — Donate buttons and fundraising](https://www.paypal.com/donate/buttons?locale.x=en_US): hosted donation pages, shareable links, and separate fundraising campaigns.

Re-check provider requirements and account eligibility at setup time; they can change and may depend on the receiving account and location.
